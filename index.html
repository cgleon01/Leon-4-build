Whats your type?

What you need a robot for is always the first question you should ask yourself before jumping into the world of physical automation. There are several types of robots that are built specifically for certain tasks.

Cleaning Robots-

The cleaning robot is designed to focus on strictly cleaning tasks. They have all the necessary tools and programs that focus on cleaning: houses, offices, and even backyards. Please note that if your room or building is complex the robot will have to spend more time learning the environment.

Cooking Robots-

Cooking can now be a thing of the past with the cooking robot. These robots are packed with all sorts of tools and programs in order to master any kitchen and produce any meal you could possibly desire. Each robot is programmed with every recipe book to ever exist but also allows you to be creative and try experimenting in order to reach your desired taste. All you have to worry about now is how hungry you are!

Security Robots

The security robot is your answer to any fears you could ever have. Monster under the bed? checked. Weird noises outside? Checked. This robot is your ultimate security system in order to give you a nice comfortable life.

    This tier takes you to the next level of capabilities with your robot. We are no longer bounded by the speeds and limits humans have. We have managed to obtain these upgrades with the implementations of fiber optic cables running through the entire robot which allows it to boost the speed of all its actions. The battery pack is only increasing in size by 10% but replaces some of its cores with our new more conductive metals. (Increase of $1,999 in total price).

    This tier is not only the best our company can offer but the best humanity as a whole is capable of. Our greatest achievements and breakthroughs all placed inside of a robot. This is no longer just a tool but now a masterpiece. An increase of movement speed of 25x faster than even the fastest human to ever exist. A battery life capable of working for 96 hours straight. That is 4 days, 4x the previous tier. We have gotten to this point by not only using our new metals but changing the whole design of the battery pack which decreases the size and weight of the battery by 15% compared to the classic robot. The use of nanotechnology decreases the overall weight of the robot and the ability for the physical materials to move faster. (Increase of $16,999 in total price).
