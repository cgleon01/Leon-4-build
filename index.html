Whats your type?

What you need a robot for is always the first question you should ask yourself before jumping into the world of physical automation. There are several types of robots that are built specifically for certain tasks.

Cleaning Robots

The cleaning robot is designed to focus on strictly cleaning tasks. They have all the necessary tools and programs that focus on cleaning: houses, offices, and even backyards. Please note that if your room or building is complex the robot will have to spend more time learning the environment.

Cooking Robots

Cooking can now be a thing of the past with the cooking robot. These robots are packed with all sorts of tools and programs in order to master any kitchen and produce any meal you could possibly desire. Each robot is programmed with every recipe book to ever exist but also allows you to be creative and try experimenting in order to reach your desired taste. All you have to worry about now is how hungry you are!

Security Robots

The security robot is your answer to any fears you could ever have. Monster under the bed? checked. Weird noises outside? Checked. This robot is your ultimate security system in order to give you a nice comfortable life.
